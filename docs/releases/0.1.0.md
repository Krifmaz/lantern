## Lantern 0.1.0

**Lantern can stand in for Prowlarr and FlareSolverr now.** Your indexers, the Cloudflare checks in front of them
and your tracker logins all live in one place, and Sonarr, Radarr, Lidarr and Readarr add Lantern as an indexer
as they would Prowlarr.

### What's in it
- **Indexers**: every site Prowlarr knows (updated daily), plus Newznab and Torznab indexers. Each one is tested,
  watched, and put on hold after repeated failures, with a notice that says which ones need you.
- **Feeds for every app**: Torznab and Newznab per indexer or for all at once at `http://<lantern>:9696/all/`,
  Prowlarr's API, and download links that never carry your API key.
- **Cloudflare checks**: Lantern's own browser gets past them by itself and keeps the site's cookies, so most
  requests need no browser. Apps that use FlareSolverr can point at Lantern's port 8191 instead.
- **Account vault**: tracker and Usenet logins, cookies, passkeys and API keys, kept encrypted.
- **Download hub**: qBittorrent and SABnzbd (also Transmission, Deluge, rTorrent and NZBGet) behind one place,
  with a folder and category for each app and callbacks when files are ready.
- **Android app**: indexers, their health and search on your phone.
